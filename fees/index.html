RERA fees

Refund, interest or possession (RERA Authority, Section 31): ₹1,000 in most states; ₹5,000 in Maharashtra (Form M in most states; Form A in Maharashtra). Compensation (Adjudicating Officer, Section 71): usually ₹5,000 (Form N in most states). Tamil Nadu charges ₹600 postage in addition. Always confirm the fee on your state portal — it is the portal's figure that counts. State-wise RERA fees →

Frequently asked questions

What is the filing fee for a claim up to ₹5 lakh?
Nil — no fee at all, under the Consumer Protection (Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions) Rules, 2020, as amended in August 2023.
Is the fee based on what I paid or what I claim?
What you paid as consideration. If you paid ₹40,000 but claim ₹5 lakh in compensation, the fee is still nil, and the forum is still the District Commission.
What is the RERA complaint fee?
₹1,000 in most states and ₹5,000 in Maharashtra for the RERA Authority (Form M / Form A); usually ₹5,000 for the Adjudicating Officer (Form N). Confirm the fee on your state portal before paying.

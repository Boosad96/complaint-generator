Sources & how we verify

Accuracy over completeness. A fee, limit or procedure ships as “verified” only when at least two independent published sources agree on it — or it comes from an official government source (a Lok Sabha answer, the portal itself, or the Act). Where sources disagree, the conflict is written down here, and the tool uses the majority value with a warning. We would rather leave a detail out than publish a wrong number for a filing.

Official sources

  • e-Jagriti portal — e-jagriti.gov.in — the unified filing portal for all Consumer Commissions since 1 January 2025 (merged e-Daakhil, OCMS, CMS and CONFONET).
  • National Consumer Helpline — consumerhelpline.gov.in — INGRAM, the pre-litigation grievance platform (1915).
  • National RERA portal — rera.gov.in — the MoHUA unified portal, use it to find your state RERA.
  • Lok Sabha answers — the revised fee slabs (2023 amendment) and the e-Jagriti / NCH programme details, as answered by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs in Parliament.

Conflicts and single-source items — shown, not hidden

State → National appeal period

Most guides say 30 days (Section 51). One guide says 45 days. The tool uses 30 days — check the order itself, which states the appeal period.

National → Supreme Court appeal period

Most guides say 30 days (Section 67). One guide says 45 days. The tool uses 30 days.

RERA 3-year limitation

Generally applied via Article 137 of the Limitation Act, as the RERA Act itself does not fix a limitation period. Shown as “generally 3 years” — file sooner rather than later.

RERA interest rate

Typically SBI MCLR + 2% per year, but state rules differ (Uttar Pradesh: MCLR + 1%). The tool never states a single rate as universal.

Rajasthan RERA form letters

One guide reports Rajasthan uses Form N for the Authority and Form O for the Adjudicating Officer, ₹1,000 each — a single source. Confirm on the Rajasthan portal before filing.

Some .gov.in RERA portals could not be reached from our server

Direct connectivity checks from this server failed for several .gov.in portals (they resolve in DNS and are confirmed by multiple published directories). All portal URLs published here are the official portals. If a link does not open for you, search “[state] RERA official” and use only .gov.in results.

Outdated numbers we deliberately do NOT use

  • District limit ₹1 crore / State ₹1–10 crore / National above ₹10 crore — the original 2019 Act figures. The 2021 Rules lowered them to ₹50 lakh / ₹2 crore. Many blogs still show the old figures; this tool uses the 2021 limits.
  • Old fee slabs (₹200 up to ₹5 lakh, ₹400 for ₹5–25 lakh…) — the pre-2023 fees. The August 2023 amendment made claims up to ₹5 lakh free and reset the slabs. This tool uses the 2023 table.
  • edaakhil.nic.in as the filing portal — e-Daakhil was merged into e-Jagriti on 1 January 2025. This tool points to e-jagriti.gov.in.

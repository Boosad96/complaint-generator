National Consumer Helpline — 1915

The free first step before any filing. The NCH is the Department of Consumer Affairs' pre-litigation grievance platform: it forwards your complaint to the company and pushes for a settlement. It costs nothing, and many refunds are settled here.

How to reach it

Phone1915 (8 AM–8 PM) or 1800-11-4000
Websiteconsumerhelpline.gov.in
WhatsApp / SMS8800001915
AlsoNCH app, UMANG app, email
Languages17 languages

What the NCH can do

  • Register your grievance and give it a unique docket number
  • Forward it to the company, regulator or ombudsman concerned
  • Show action updates against your docket
  • Remind the company at set intervals
  • Help both sides try a pre-litigation settlement (usually within about 30 days)

1,398 companies in the Convergence programme respond directly respond directly to grievances on the portal.

What it cannot do

  • Order a refund, replacement or compensation
  • Impose a binding penalty on the company
  • Execute a refund order
  • Guarantee any remedy — if it does not work, the next step is the Consumer Commission

If the company does not resolve it — usually within about 30 days — the next step is the Consumer Commission on e-Jagriti. Find your route →

Frequently asked questions

What is the National Consumer Helpline number?
1915 (8 AM–8 PM) or 1800-11-4000. You can also file at consumerhelpline.gov.in, or by WhatsApp/SMS on 8800001915.
Can the NCH order a refund?
No. The NCH is a pre-litigation platform — it forwards your grievance to the company and pushes for a settlement, usually within about 30 days. If that fails, file with the Consumer Commission on e-Jagriti.
Does the NCH charge anything?
No. Registering a grievance with the NCH is free.

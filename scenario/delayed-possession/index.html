Builder delayed flat possession — RERA complaint

A promoter who fails to hand over possession by the date agreed in the agreement for sale.

RERA route. File on your state RERA portal — not on e-Jagriti. The portal is picked by where the property is. State portals, forms & fees →

The possession date in your agreement has passed and the builder keeps pushing it. Under RERA you can stay and claim interest for the delay, or withdraw and take a refund with interest.

What you can get

Possession with interest for the delay, or withdrawal with a full refund plus interest (typically SBI MCLR + 2% per year; state rules differ). Compensation is decided separately by the Adjudicating Officer.

Steps

  1. Check that the project is RERA-registered on your state RERA portal — this route only exists for registered projects.
  2. Write to the builder demanding possession or a revised date, and keep the reply.
  3. Decide what you want: stay and claim interest for the delay, or withdraw and claim a full refund with interest (Section 18).
  4. File with the RERA Authority on your state portal — Form M in most states (Form A in Maharashtra). Fee ₹1,000 in most states, ₹5,000 in Maharashtra.
  5. If you also want compensation beyond interest or refund, file separately with the Adjudicating Officer (Form N, usually ₹5,000).
  6. The Authority's disposal target is 60 days. Either side can appeal to the Real Estate Appellate Tribunal within 60 days of the order.

Documents to prepare

  • Agreement for sale (with the promised possession date)
  • Payment receipts and bank statements
  • Allotment letter
  • RERA project registration details
  • Letters / emails to the builder demanding possession
  • Any revised possession letters from the builder

Frequently asked questions

Should I file with the RERA Authority or the Adjudicating Officer?
Refund, interest or possession go to the RERA Authority (Section 31, usually Form M). Compensation for loss — beyond interest — goes to the Adjudicating Officer (Section 71, usually Form N). Many homebuyers file both.
What interest rate applies?
Typically SBI MCLR + 2% per year. State rules differ — for example, Uttar Pradesh links it to SBI MCLR + 1%. Your state portal shows the rate it applies.
Is there a time limit?
Generally 3 years from the cause of action (Limitation Act, Article 137, as applied by the courts). The RERA Act itself does not fix a period — file sooner rather than later.
